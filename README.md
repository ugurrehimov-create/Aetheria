# AstraLog
Look up on a clear night and the sky feels like a question you have always been almost able to answer. AstraLog is a place to follow that feeling: to travel across the real sky, mark the stars and worlds that stay with you, and ask whether a planet light-years away could hold an ocean. Every answer is worked out in the open, so you can see exactly how we know what we know.
It was made by someone who wanted company under the stars. If you have ever felt small and grateful at the same moment while looking at the universe, you belong here. Soon, we will look up together.
